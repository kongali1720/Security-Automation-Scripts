# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free environment.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on collaboration.
- Help newcomers.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Spam
- Hate speech

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
